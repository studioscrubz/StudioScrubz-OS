# StudioScrubz operational standards — v1.0
Management-approved policy baseline. Chemical formulas are NOT released for employee use until actual SKU labels and SDS are reviewed and signed off. Production deployment of the integrated inventory implementation is authorized after applicable release checks pass.

## SOP-KIT-001 — Technician kit and readiness
Crew Lead owns crew readiness; solo technician owns own readiness. All checks and maintenance occur on paid time.
Everyday essentials (target / restock at): clean microfiber 100/35; trash bags 20/5; gloves 40/10 pairs; shoe covers 10/5 pairs; paper towels 2/1 rolls.
Microfiber color (target/threshold): red toilets 15/5; yellow bathroom sinks/showers 15/5; green kitchen 15/5; blue glass/mirrors 15/5; orange general surfaces/furniture/dusting 30/10; gray stainless steel 10/5. Trigger on any color or aggregate threshold; separate dirty and clean, launder without softener.
Standard: bucket 5 gal, organizer, transport cart, four dedicated 24 fl oz spray bottles (disinfectant, glass, alcohol, degreaser), wood cleaner, air freshener, toilet bowl cleaner, Dawn Powerwash, two Scrub Daddy sponges, floor steamer, vacuum. One each except sponges. Deep add-on: scraper, Pink Stuff paste, Bar Keepers Friend liquid (one each). Specialty as needed: shower, mold/mildew and grout cleaners (one each), grout brushes and steel wool cleaning towels (two each proposed). Total 27 item categories, excluding bulk/refill stock. Standard = essentials + standard; deep and move-in/out add deep; specialty only when needed. Check Equipment verifies presence, adequate stock, labels, operational equipment, applicable specialty scope. Missing required or unsafe items block readiness; notify management. Post-job clean equipment, separate towels, replenish and report defects.

## SOP-CHM-001 — Chemical preparation and safety
Four 24 oz bottles, top-mounted durable text labels; NO bottle color coding. Include chemical identity, dilution, hazard information as applicable, preparation date and initials. Original bulk containers retain labels.
Management-approved candidate formulas (release gate: verify exact product label, SDS, compatible container and use; do not publish as usable until verified):
- Zep Antibacterial Disinfectant with Lemon: 24 oz undiluted ONLY if exact label confirms ready-to-use; contact time per label.
- Dawn Ultra Original: 1/4 teaspoon, water to 24 oz total; internal glass cleaner, not disinfectant.
- 90% isopropyl rubbing alcohol, any brand without dyes/fragrance/oils: 18 2/3 fl oz, water to 24 fl oz total, nominal 70%; flammable, cleaner not validated disinfectant. Different strength requires review.
- Zep Heavy-Duty Citrus Degreaser (replaces Industrial Purple): candidate light 1.41 oz +22.59 oz water, moderate 12+12, heavy 24+0; light default. Verify actual SKU dilution directions before release; moderate/heavy only on compatible surfaces when warranted.
- Zep Neutral Floor Cleaner ZUNEUT128: candidate 1/2/3–5 fl oz per gallon water for light/normal/heavy respectively; confirm label. No chemical in steamer unless manufacturer permits.
Bulk target/threshold: Zep disinfectant, citrus, floor cleaner each 128/32 fl oz; Dawn Ultra 56/14 fl oz; alcohol any qualifying container, 25% remaining. Other bottled/paste products target one, threshold 25%. Refill bottles locally, request bulk replenishment.
Refill kit: chemical-resistant measuring cup, spoon set, funnel, compatible gloves, splash goggles, labels, marker, containment tray; four bottles already counted. Dedicated measuring tools. Ventilated area, PPE per SDS, verify formula, measure, prepare per label, secure and label, store upright, separate incompatibles, clean tools, check stock. Never mix different cleaners; keep alcohol away from ignition, heat and steamers. No food containers or unauthorized substitution. SDS accessible; report spills/exposures; eye/skin rinse per SDS, fresh air for inhalation, poison control 1-800-222-1222, emergency 911. No neutralizing mixtures. Trainer observes product identification, preparation, PPE, labels, storage, SDS, incident response and restocking; management sign-off before independent preparation.

## SOP-EQM-001 — Equipment quality and maintenance
Bucket no leaks/cracks, organizer secure, cart stable wheels/handles, bottles labeled and leak-free, sponges/brushes intact, towels clean/dry, steamer pads clean and electrical components intact, vacuum suction and attachments functional, scraper blade protected and intact, steel towels rust-free and compatible with surface, measuring tools clean and legible. Remove unsafe equipment from service. After each job empty vacuum, inspect attachments, clean steamer pads, separate towels, clean tools, wipe bottles and bucket, replenish. End day clean bucket/cart, check labels and bulk levels, count clean stock, request replenishment, secure storage. Weekly inspect filters/brush roll/hose/cord, steamer seals/reservoir/cord, cart fasteners, organizer, bottle seals, blades and prep tools per manufacturer. Monthly manager condition review. Condition statuses excellent/good/fair/needs maintenance/out of service/missing. Repair or replace when damaged, worn, ineffective or unsafe; no unauthorized repairs. Report issue, photo if applicable, notify manager, obtain repair/replacement and confirm safe return. Track asset ID, brand/model/serial where available, purchase date/cost, assignee, issuance/return, condition and maintenance history. Example IDs EQP-VAC-001, EQP-STM-001, EQP-CRT-001, KIT-TECH-001. Consumables quantity tracked.

## SOP-INV-001 — Inventory and restocking
Central stock, crew assigned stock, solo assigned stock; company property. Track consumables by quantity, chemicals by volume, reusable tools by quantity/condition and durable assets individually. At or below threshold replenish to target (max(0,target-current)); above threshold still ensure enough for upcoming work. Requests record item, current amount, requested quantity, reason, priority, assignee. Status submitted, approved, awaiting purchase, ready for distribution, issued, received, completed, declined, cancelled. Routine/next assignment/urgent current job. Prevent duplicate open shortages. Manager approves, prepares/purchases, issues; employee acknowledges; reconcile stock atomically. No personal purchases without prior authorization. Operations Manager and Master Admin approve/issue/purchase/assign/repair; Master Admin controls standards, approved products and disposal. Crew Lead sees own crew; solo sees own; managers see all. Transactions purchase/issue/consumption/return/transfer/adjustment/laundry return/damage/loss/disposal; record actor, reason, timestamps, quantities and locations. Pre/post job and end day employee checks; weekly manager shortage review, monthly count, quarterly admin expense review, offboarding return. Central stocking targets not yet established.

## Implementation acceptance criteria
Reuse existing Check Equipment, jobs, service types, employees, roles, onboarding, training, time clock, notifications, profitability and equipment records. No duplicate systems. Dynamic checklist with assigned responsible person, versioned standards, conditional specialty items, missing/unsafe blocks, audit history and compensated time. Operations navigation: Inventory Dashboard, Master Inventory, Kit Standards, Assigned Kits, Equipment Inspections, Restocking Requests, Chemical Formula Library, Equipment Maintenance, Inventory Transactions. Formula library read-only for technicians, management version control, SDS and release gates. Training competency and equipment issuance acknowledgement. Stock transfers transactional, idempotent, no negative stock, permission enforcement server-side. Test standard/deep/solo/crew, thresholds, roles, concurrency, missing items, and chemical release gate. Do not merge or deploy without separate approval.
